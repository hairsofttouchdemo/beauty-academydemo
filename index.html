01 — Our House

Two doors, one standard.

SAARAAZ is a beauty salon and a professional training academy. Clients come to us for results they feel proud of; aspiring artists come to learn how those results are made.

We believe both deserve the same care: time to listen, honest advice, clean and considered work, and an atmosphere that feels warm the moment you walk in.

Our founding story, team profiles and milestones will be shared here soon, in our own words.

I

Story

Built around a simple belief: beauty work should make people feel seen, and those who do it should be properly taught.

II

Vision

To be a place clients trust with their important days, and where new artists find the grounding to build lasting careers.

III

Training

Practical, hands-on learning with guidance at every step — technique, hygiene, client care and professional conduct.

IV

Quality

Careful consultation, clean tools, honest recommendations and finishing that holds up beyond the mirror.
